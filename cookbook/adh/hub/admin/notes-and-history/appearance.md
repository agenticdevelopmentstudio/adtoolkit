
- **Corner radius**: Notes-list items: 6px rounded corners. History-list
  items: none — flat text lines with no container.
- **Padding**: Root container: 16px top margin, 16px gap between the two
  sections. Notes-list items: 8px padding on all sides; the notes list:
  4px top margin, 8px gap between items. The history list: 4px top margin,
  4px gap between items.
- **Font**: Section headings: 12px, semibold, uppercase, wide letter
  spacing. Loading/empty messages and list content: 14px. Note author/date
  line and history timestamp: 12px.
- **Background**: Notes-list items: the surface (secondary) theme role at
  40% opacity. History-list items and the root container: transparent (no
  background).
- **Foreground/Text**: Section headings: muted-text theme role. Note
  content and history line text: primary-text theme role. Note author/date
  and history timestamp: muted-text theme role. Loading and empty messages:
  dim-text theme role.
- **Border**: Notes-list items: 1px border, border theme role color.
  History-list items: none.
- **Shadow**: None — no shadow appears anywhere in the design.
- **Min/Max size**: None — no width/height constraint is applied; the
  section grows to fit its content.
